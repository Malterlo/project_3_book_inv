Why These Books?

I chose these books to match what I’m curious about right now: stories, history, nature, and data. Fantasy and science fiction like The Hobbit, Dune, and Piranesi keep my imagination active, while Siddhartha gives me space to think about purpose. On the nonfiction side, When the War Was Over helps me understand Cambodia’s past, and Storytelling with Data connects directly to my studies in data and information systems.

My next two are Piranesi and Braiding Sweetgrass. Piranesi comes first because it’s a lighter, imaginative read after a dense history book. Braiding Sweetgrass is second because it speaks to my interest in conservation and science communication, and I want to learn how to write about nature in a way that makes people care. Together they balance escape with purpose.
